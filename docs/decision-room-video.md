# Decision Room video

This portfolio teaser was created to support a public invitation to stress-test **Decision Room: Authority Under Pressure**. The concept tests whether a replacement team can establish authority, boundaries, escalation, and risk ownership during the first five minutes of a serious cyber incident.

The scene is fictional and illustrative. It is not a record of a real breach and does not give operational response instructions. The video is a supporting creative artifact, not a feature demonstration of the Governed Agent MCP application.

- Narration draft: [voiceover script](../demo/decision-room-video/voiceover.txt)
