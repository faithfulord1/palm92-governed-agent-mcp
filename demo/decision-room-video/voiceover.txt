THE FIRST FIVE MINUTES

The phone rings. Nobody answers.
A privileged account is behaving strangely. The evidence is incomplete.
Group says wait. The subsidiary says act. Both see a real risk.
The CISO is unreachable. The clock keeps moving.
Who has authority? What can happen now? When do we escalate? Who owns the remaining risk?
A plan is useful. A decision path must work.
Stress-test authority under pressure, before a real incident does it for you.
